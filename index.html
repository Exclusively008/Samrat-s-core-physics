Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Samrat Core Physics

What subjects do you teach?
We specialize in Physics for students from Class 9 to Class 12, covering all board syllabi (WBSE, CBSE, ICSE) and competitive exam preparation (JEE, WBJEE, NEET Physics).
How many students are there in each batch?
We maintain small batch sizes of 15-20 students to ensure personalized attention and effective learning.
What is the fee structure?
Our fees are ₹700/month for all classes (9 to 12). This includes regular classes, study materials, test series, and doubt-clearing sessions.
Do you provide study materials?
Yes, we provide comprehensive study materials including chapter-wise notes, practice problems, previous year question papers, and mock tests.
How can I enroll?
You can enroll by filling the admission form on our website, or by contacting us directly via phone or WhatsApp at +91 89816 38647.
Is there a trial class available?
No. Contact us to experience our teaching methodology firsthand.
